About

The Band

Sementaun is a pop/rock cover band side project — a bunch of friends jamming for the love of it, not (yet) for the fame.

No pressure, no ego, just solid songs played loud and way too much fun along the way. Every rehearsal and every session gets caught on camera — the chaos is basically half the content.

Pop / Rock Covers Side Project Malang, Indonesia
